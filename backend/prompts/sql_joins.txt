Rules for reading several tables:
- Join tables only on the relationships listed above, with exactly those columns. Never join on other columns, even when their names look alike.
- When a query reads more than one table, write every column with its table name or alias, for example orders."order_id" or o."order_id".
- "many A rows per B row" means one row of B matches many rows of A. Before SUM or AVG of a column of B over a join with A, aggregate A first in a subquery or WITH table with one row per join key, then join; otherwise every value of B is counted once per matching row of A. Count rows of B with COUNT(DISTINCT key).
- Read only the tables the question needs.
- Two tables that no chain of the relationships above connects cannot be joined. If the question needs both, reply with unanswerable and say in the reason that the two tables have no relationship in the database.
